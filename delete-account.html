Rosvon provides an in-app deletion flow and an external email request path. The current in-app flow attempts several local, Google Drive, Firebase, and Google-account operations; it is not instant, can fail, and does not yet remove every UID-linked backend transaction/audit record.

RECOMMENDED (METHOD 1)

📱 Delete Directly from Within the App

This flow attempts to delete a locally identified Drive backup, supported Firebase/Storage records, Firebase Authentication account, user-linked purchase-token, billing-event, and void-event records, and app-private local data. It requires network/provider access and may require retry or support. Completed deletion is permanent.

  1. Open the Rosvon app on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to the Help & Support section from the main navigation menu or drawer.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account & Data".
  4. Read the confirmation dialog showing what data will be removed.
  5. While signed in, type the displayed account email or DELETE phrase to confirm. The current App does not perform a fresh Google credential reauthentication.
  6. Tap "Confirm Delete". Wait for the result. If Drive, Firebase, Auth, or network work fails, retry or contact support. Do not assume all data is gone until the App reports completion and the current exceptions below have been handled.
METHOD 2

📧 Request Account Deletion via Email

If you cannot access the app or prefer email assistance, you can submit an account deletion request to our support team.

Send an email request:

📧 Email: rosvon.support@gmail.com

📌 Subject: "Account Deletion Request — Rosvon App"

📝 Include: Your registered email address and username so we can locate your account.

⏱️ Processing time depends on identity verification, provider access, and applicable law; the App does not guarantee completion within 48 hours.

⚠️ Email request scope: Email is a request for manual handling of supported Firebase/Auth/Storage account data. It cannot erase app-private data on your phone or your Drive App Data files. Use Rosvon's in-app flow, Android clear-data/uninstall, and Google Account storage controls as applicable. The current backend exceptions listed below must also be handled before server deletion can be described as complete.

Subscriptions and Requests for Young Users

Deleting your Rosvon account or uninstalling the App does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately in Google Play to stop future renewal. Refund and other statutory rights remain subject to applicable law and Google Play policy.

A parent or lawful guardian may request deletion for a young user by emailing rosvon.support@gmail.com with the subject Guardian Account Deletion Request — Rosvon, the account email or username if known, and a short description of the request. We may need to verify identity and authority before disclosing or deleting account data. Do not email passwords, payment details, or government identity documents.

Guest Mode (Local Data) Note

If you use Rosvon in Guest Mode without signing in, clearing App storage or uninstalling removes Rosvon's app-private local records. Google Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance, Mobile Ads, Play, Android, or other provider-held records are outside the local App sandbox and follow provider retention.
